# The Celebration Bureau

I am building a consumer web app called The Celebration Bureau - You choose the occasion, we take care of the rest.



The product is an AI-powered party concierge.



For V1, we are ONLY supporting children's princess birthday parties.



Target customer:

US parents planning a birthday party for a child aged 4–8.



Typical party:

8–15 children

Home or backyard

Budget $150–$300



Core promise:

"You choose the theme. We take care of the rest."



The product should feel premium, calm, feminine, modern and editorial.

Think sophisticated children's party planning rather than a childish party website.



Visual direction:

warm ivory

soft blush

sage

muted gold

warm taupe

lots of whitespace

editorial photography

subtle rounded cards

premium but approachable



Do NOT make it look like a generic SaaS dashboard.

Do NOT use childish cartoon graphics.

Do NOT overuse pink.



For the first prototype, create these screens:



1. Landing page

2. Party Builder

3. Free Party Preview

4. Checkout placeholder

5. Party Dashboard

6. To Do

7. Shopping List

8. Activities

9. Food



The app must be mobile-first but also work beautifully on desktop.



Do not implement AI yet.

Do not create fake random party data.

Use one realistic example party:

Emma, age 7, 10 children, backyard, $200 budget, elegant magical princess theme, low DIY.



Build the frontend and navigation first.
